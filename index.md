# About the game

*It's you versus a pack of fast and clever killer cars*

Welcome to Red Mist Pines. An All-American mountain town caught in a crippling snowstorm. The power is failing, the town is plunged into darkness and vicious killer cars are moving in the shadows. Supercars charge at breakneck speed, unmarked vans lurk in the shadows and heavy-duty trucks stop at nothing to hunt you down. Prepare for nerve-wracking chases where one wrong move could be your last. Dash across wide parking lots, maze-like suburbs and desolate mountain roads. Try to make it out alive or stay and fight back.

Decimate Drive 2 builds on the fast-paced gameplay of the original with bigger locations to explore, larger groups of enemies that work together, and more ways to defend yourself.

## About the franchise

Decimate Drive is inspired by classic killer car horror movies such as Duel (1971) and Christine (1983). By pitting players against killer cars, the game delivers hectic unscripted action without ever compromising on its dark atmospheric horror setting.

The original was released in early 2024 and received a very positive rating from players on Steam and caught the attention of numerous streamers including smii7y, ManlyBadassHero, AstralSpiff and CaseOh.

## Development roadmap

Decimate Drive 2 is currently in early development, with a release planned for Q4 2027. A public playtest is planned for Q1 2027, so that players can try the game early and help improve it.

# About Some Random Designing

Some Random Designing is a Dutch solo indie developer behind the frantic Decimate Drive and the relaxing Babbling Brook:

> “I enjoy taking somewhat unusual or random ideas and turning them into fully fledged games. After creating a fun cozy game around water physics I’m excited to switch back to the polar opposite with a sequel to Decimate Drive. With fresh ideas and loads of player feedback, I can’t wait to see where we can take this random idea next.”
